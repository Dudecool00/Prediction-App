# Dated injury comparison protocol

Declared October 15, 2026 before the first fits using the dated news counts. This follows
inspection of earlier EPA/context results and outcome-free 2023-2024 source coverage;
it is adaptive development, not an untouched holdout. The PEM-inspired comparison remains
after completion of the original replay plan.

1. Qualify the separately collected 2022 canonical news archive with the unchanged article
   parser, integrity checks, latest-whole-edition rules and forecast cutoffs. Use canonical
   editions uniformly across every 2022 week; keep the existing qualified canonical/tracking
   2023-2024 audit intact. Unknown positions and missing reports remain unknown. This source
   clarification was recorded before any real dated-count fits: one of 41 initialization
   tracking indexes remained unavailable after bounded retries, so none of those tracking
   editions enters initialization. This is a declared source sample, not complete injury coverage.
2. Reuse the 2022-initialized Tuesday-locked EPA table. Compare compact EPA (8 inputs),
   compact EPA plus five availability flags (13), and those inputs plus two Out-count
   contrasts (15). The flags are each team's formal-report/known-position indicators plus
   a both-position-known indicator. The contrasts are home minus away offensive
   (QB+OL+skill) Out counts and defensive Out counts. Both teams must have known positions.
   Otherwise contrasts are neutral zero with their availability indicators retained in
   both context candidates; automatic missingness indicators cannot create an unmatched
   archive-coverage feature. A listed QB Out is not a confirmed starting-QB absence.
3. All models use the same alpha-20 two-output Ridge, training-only preprocessing, all
   development games, identical Tuesday fits and common fractional-score/margin contract.
   Require exact agreement with the saved compact-EPA control. Keep 2022 initialization,
   2023-2024 development, no pre-2022 history, no within-week result updates and zero 2025 fits.
4. Formal reports have zero Tuesday coverage in the outcome-free development audit, so
   the primary injury-effect assessment is pre-kickoff refresh. Against both EPA and
   availability-only, require at least 0.05 margin-MAE gain and a positive approximate
   95% season/week-block gain lower bound (5,000 draws, seed 20261015). Score MAE may regress
   at most 0.05 and total MAE at most 0.10 at either horizon; neither horizon's overall
   margin may regress, and a single season's margin may regress at most 0.25. The fallback
   is compact EPA. Availability-only cannot itself become the selected forecast model.
5. Simulate selection on 2023 before assessing every 2024 game. Separately record a
   pre-2025 research choice from 2023-2024, with actual recording time and simulated
   September 2, 2025 lock. Both-covered games are a labeled secondary sensitivity only.
   These are examined development seasons; separation does not remove earlier access.
6. Store only context deltas, one prediction panel and aggregate evidence. Preserve
   original choices and source bytes. This limited research test cannot clear the full
   enriched-replay gate: dated active/IR rosters, confirmed starters, and full historical
   forecast weather/adapters remain separate requirements. No silent readiness bypass.
